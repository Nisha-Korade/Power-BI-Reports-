Music Streaming Analytics Dashboard — Power BI<br><br>
📊 Overview<br>

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing listener, subscription, genre, and platform data across a music streaming service, with Artist and Top Genre filters.<br><br>

🔑 Features<br>
KPI Cards — Premium Subscribers (1.193K), Total Listeners (4K), Total Artists (14), Total Genres (12)<br>
Playlists Count & Listening Minutes by Platform — Amazon, Apple Music, Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube Music, Tidal<br>
Subscription Distribution — Free, Premium, Family, Student split<br>
Platform Popularity — Listener count by platform<br>
Genre Analysis — Donut chart across Pop, Hip-Hop, EDM, Rock, R&B, Indie, K-Pop, Afrobeats, Lo-fi, Country, Classical, Jazz<br>
Artist-Genre Matrix — Table cross-tabbing artists (Travis Scott, Taylor Swift, Drake, etc.) against genre counts<br>
Listeners by Country — Japan, Canada, USA, Brazil, Nigeria, Australia<br>
Slicers — Filter by Artist and Top Genre<br><br>

💡 Key Insights<br>
Free tier dominates at 45.5% of subscriptions — conversion to paid plans is a growth opportunity<br>
Spotify leads platform popularity by a wide margin over Apple Music and YouTube Music<br>
Pop (18%) and Hip-Hop (15%) are top genres, while Jazz and Classical are niche (~3–4%)<br>
USA has the highest listener base, followed closely by Japan and Canada<br>
Artist-genre table shows Travis Scott and Taylor Swift with the highest combined genre engagement<br><br>
🎯 Purpose<br><br>

Enables quick, self-service answers to:<br>

Which platforms and genres drive the most engagement?<br>
How is the subscriber base split across plan types?<br>
Which countries and artists contribute most to listenership?<br><br>

📈 Business Value<br>
Identifies upsell opportunity from Free to Premium/Family plans<br>
Highlights top-performing platforms and genres for content/licensing decisions<br>
Supports region-specific marketing based on listener concentration<br>
Helps prioritize artist partnerships based on cross-genre popularity<br><br>

Financial DataSet Dashboard — Power BI<br><br>
<p>📊 Overview<br>

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing company-wide sales, profit, cost (COGS), and customer segments across 5 countries for 2013–2014, with Year (dropdown) and Quarter-level filtering.<br><br>

🔑 Features<br>
KPI Cards — Total Profit (13.02M) & Total Sales (92.31M)<br>
COGS by Country — Cost distribution across US, Canada, France, Germany, Mexico<br>
Product by Segment — Customer mix across Government, Enterprise, Midmarket, Small Business, Channel Partners<br>
Profit by Month — Seasonal trend with drill-down tooltips<br>
Sales by Country — Country-wise revenue ranking<br>
Slicers — Year dropdown (2013, 2014) and Quarter buttons<br><br>
💡 Key Insights<br>
Profit margin is only ~14% of sales — cost structure needs review<br>
US drives ~80% of total sales — high market concentration risk<br>
COGS is spread evenly across countries despite uneven sales — smaller markets are cost-inefficient<br>
Government segment = 43% of product mix — exposes revenue to budget/policy cycles<br>
Profit peaks in Q4 (Oct–Dec) — likely tied to fiscal year-end spending<br><br>
🎯 Purpose<br>

Enables quick, self-service answers to:<br>

How profitable is the business, and where?<br>
Which markets/segments drive or drag performance?<br>
When does the business perform best/worst?<br>
📈 Business Value<br>
Faster, data-driven decisions without manual reporting<br>
Identifies risk concentration (market + segment)<br>
Supports cash-flow and seasonal planning<br>
Executive-ready snapshot for reviews<br><br>
🛠️ Tools Used<br>

Power BI · DAX · Data Modeling · Interactive Visualization
